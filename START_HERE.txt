🎯 KEY FINDINGS:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

What increases repair time?
1. Weather (rain/snow +30-65%)
2. Failure type (track circuits take 71 mins vs 39 mins for software)
3. Temperature (extreme cold/heat harder)
4. Time of day (peak hours +10% longer)
